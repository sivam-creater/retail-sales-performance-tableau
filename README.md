📊 Retail Sales Performance Dashboard — Tableau

📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Retail Sales Performance Dashboard created using Tableau.

The dashboard provides an overview of sales, profit, orders, customers, product performance, category performance, regional sales, customer segments, and monthly sales trends.

---
🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze retail sales performance and identify important trends and patterns that can support business decision-making.

📈 Dashboard Analysis

Monthly Sales Trend

Analyzed monthly sales performance to identify changes and trends throughout the year.

Sales by Category

Compared sales performance across:

- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Technology

Profit by Category

Analyzed profit contribution across different product categories.

Top 10 Products

Identified the top-performing products based on sales.

Sales by Region

Used a geographical visualization to analyze sales performance across regions.

Sales by Customer Segment

Compared sales across:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

---
💡 Key Insights

- Total sales reached approximately **$2.33M**.
- Total profit was approximately **$292.30K**.
- The dashboard recorded **5,111 orders** from **804 customers**.
- Technology generated the highest sales among the three categories shown.
- Consumer represented the largest customer segment by sales.
- The dashboard highlights monthly fluctuations in sales performance.
- The Top 10 Products view identifies products contributing strongly to overall sales.
